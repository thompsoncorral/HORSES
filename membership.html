Become a Member

Membership is how congregants formally join the life of H.O.R.S.E.S. Church — its worship, its teaching, and its equine sanctuary work.

Membership in H.O.R.S.E.S. Church is free, voluntary, and open to all. We see everyone and all things as one divine community, and we believe rescuing creatures great and small is a universal act of love.

Ways to Belong

Active Member

Ordained ministers, clergy, staff, educators, organizers, and volunteers working to rescue equines, record births, or keep covenants in the Sacred Ecclesiastical Registrum.

Lifetime Member

Anyone who files or records covenants for their equines in the church Registrum may request lifetime membership.

Honorary Member

Donors, and those leaving gifts or bequests to the church in their wills.

Supporting Member

Horse owners, animal and nature lovers, followers of mystical union or the Finished Works theology, and anyone who supports our mission.

Our horses are members too. Every equine recorded in the Registrum, and every Sainted equine, is automatically a lifetime member.

You don't need to be a member to…

  • Create or submit covenants to the church archives
  • Make a donation
  • Partner with us to save horses and advocate for sanctuaries
