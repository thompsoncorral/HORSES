The Theology of H.O.R.S.E.S. Church

An omni-faith centered on divine union with the One, and the sacred duty of those who care and minister for equines and all that is.

Mystical Union

All things are extensions of the one godliness

▾

All living things are woven from one unifying godliness — not separate from the divine, but part of it.

The Sacred Covenant

A spiritual commitment for the horse's lifetime

Read more about the sacred covenant ▾

▾
A promise that runs with the horse. A covenant protects a horse for its entire life.

How a covenant works

  1. A church member makes a solemn promise — a vow to protect and perform — for a horse.
  2. The covenant is recorded and permanently stored in the H.O.R.S.E.S. Church ecclesiastical Registrum.
  3. The promise attaches to the horse itself, not the person.
  4. Every future owner or caretaker is bound to honor it, for the whole of the horse’s life — regardless of their faith or membership.

A covenant does

  • Travel with the horse forever
  • Bind everyone who comes after
  • Stand as a lawful, recorded instrument

A covenant does not

  • Bind the church
  • Bind the member who makes it
  • Force anyone to acquire a covenanted horse

Example

A member may covenant that a horse may never leave the USA or be sold for human consumption.
